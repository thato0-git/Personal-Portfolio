## 🎯 How to Apply Your Chosen Palette:

1. **Copy the CSS variables** from your chosen palette
2. **Replace the variables** in the `:root` section of `style.css` (lines 12-20)
3. **Save the file** and refresh your browser
4. **Done!** Your entire website will update with the new color scheme
